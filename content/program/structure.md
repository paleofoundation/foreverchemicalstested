# Program structure

Forever Chemicals Tested proposes a standard per product category with scheduled retesting. Certification applies to a defined product, category, analyte scope, standard edition and surveillance schedule. It does not establish that a product is free of all PFAS or that a measured result guarantees safety.

This draft is for review. It has no effective date, approved numerical ICS ceiling, issued certificate or licensed mark. Category pages are in the [standards register](../standards/index.md).

## Entry and category assignment

The applicant identifies the product form, composition, intended use, production process, food-contact materials and intended markets. The operator maps that product to the exact category and applicable regulatory provisions. Separate tissue, formulation and material definitions remain separate. An ingredient standard does not automatically govern a finished composite product.

The operator reviews baseline analytical evidence, method capability, traceability and process controls. The baseline sampling design and acceptance decision must be approved for the PFAS matrix before entry. A single convenient passing result cannot substitute for the required sampling design. Existing metal-panel requirements are not imported into this program.

## Conformance and status

A proposed certification decision requires all applicable category requirements to be satisfied. This includes product-use prohibitions, source-scope conditions, approved numerical criteria where available, a complete analytical panel and an assigned retest schedule. A blank numeric cell is not a passing result. Categories with unresolved source scope or decision rules remain on HOLD.

The draft lifecycle follows the template's product-level statuses: Certified, Remediation, Suspension and Revocation. Only Certified permits mark use. Corrective action or missing evidence does not authorize continued mark use. The operator records the reason, evidence, decision owner, affected product scope and effective date for each status change. Reinstatement requires verified corrective action and a new documented decision.

## Governance and evidence separation

The Institute of Contaminant Standards governs the proposed program. The [method](method.md) controls derivation and provenance. The [retesting policy](retesting.md) controls surveillance. [Licensee terms](licensee-terms.md) define the proposed obligations.

The certification program may cite [Forever Chemicals Index](https://foreverchemicalsindex.com) for contextual research. Research occurrence does not set a regulatory maximum or determine a certificate. The index does not promote certification. No index content or records are changed by this draft.

Applications are intended to use the central Institute of Contaminant Standards certification hub. This draft adds no application form, account, payment flow, applicant data or operational hub route. The hub remains separate queued work.
