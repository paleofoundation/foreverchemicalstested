# Scheduled retesting

Every activated category standard must include an assigned calendar schedule. The operator records the product category, risk assessment, method and matrix capability, sampling unit, responsible laboratory, interval, last accepted test and next due date. A product with no approved schedule is not eligible for certification.

The read-only template's Category 1 manual, Part 4 sections 4.2, 4.4 and 4.9, supplies candidate surveillance intervals: monthly for High-Risk and quarterly for Moderate-/Lower-Risk products. These intervals are documented template provisions. PFAS risk tiers have not been assigned and are not inferred from metal risk tiers. G-09 must resolve the category assignment before an interval becomes binding.

Retesting uses a documented sampling plan suited to the product's formulation, source variation, manufacturing controls and regulatory measurement basis. The operator reviews laboratory capability before approving the plan. Insufficient analyte coverage, quantification limits above an approved criterion, missing uncertainty or missing matrix validation prevents acceptance of the evidence.

A change in formulation, ingredient source, supplier, water source, process or product-contact material triggers review before relying on the prior certification scope. An adverse result, complaint or regulatory change can trigger additional testing and a revised schedule. The operator documents the reason and due date. There is no universal production-lot coverage claim.

A missed scheduled test, unresolved exceedance or material evidence failure triggers a status review and withholding or removal of the mark. Corrective action and confirmatory testing follow a documented plan. Repeated selective sampling to obtain a favorable result cannot establish conformance. Return to Certified requires operator approval of the corrective action and the complete evidence set.
