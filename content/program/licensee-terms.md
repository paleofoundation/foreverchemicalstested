# Licensee terms

These proposed obligations require final program approval and an executed license. This draft offers no license or certificate, and sets no fee, term length or commercial deadline.

The licensee identifies each covered product, its exact category, manufacturing and supply-chain controls, intended market and standard edition. Certification is limited to that approved scope. The licensee supplies complete laboratory records, sampling records, method versions, quantification limits, uncertainties and chain of custody for review. Record retention and access procedures must be agreed before activation.

The licensee follows the assigned schedule and provides evidence by its recorded due date. Changes that could affect PFAS content or the applicable category must be disclosed for review. Missing or unfavorable findings must not be omitted from the evidence set. Intentional-use prohibitions require appropriate formulation and supply-chain evidence in addition to analytical testing.

Mark use is permitted only while the covered product is Certified. Remediation, Suspension and Revocation carry no permission to present the affected product as certified. The operator documents corrective-action requirements and the basis for any reinstatement. A licensee must cooperate with applicable regulatory reporting and recall duties. Confidentiality does not override those duties.

Claims must state the applicable category standard and scope. They must not imply absence of all PFAS, universal safety, broader product coverage or testing coverage beyond the approved sampling plan. Regulatory compliance and certification are distinct decisions, and a draft source table is neither decision.

Final license language, appeals, confidentiality, records access, retention, termination and mark-use procedures remain G-10. Applications are intended for the central ICS hub; this repository provides no separate intake workflow.
